# References

Reference images plus a note on what to take from each **and what not to take**. The second half matters more than the first — an unqualified reference produces a copy, not a design.

---

## `00-hero-approved.png` — the approved hero

Not a reference. This is the client-approved home page hero and the origin of the system's colour, radius, and layout direction.

**Take:** the full-bleed dusk photograph, the single generously rounded container floating over it, the left copy / right portrait card split, the proof figures along the bottom, pill controls.

**Note:** the hero as originally built used a sans throughout and a sage green CTA. Both were resolved into the system — its headline is now Playfair like every other h1, and the green is gone.

---

## `01-curve-and-figures.png` — statistic cards over a curved seam

**Take:**
- The curved transition where a light band resolves into a dark photographic one
- Figures presented on panels that straddle the seam, so one element belongs to both bands
- Big number, small label, no decoration between them

**Do not take:**
- The gold accent — this project is one blue
- The icon above every number; the figure is the information, an icon on top of it is decoration
- A "Learn More" button repeated on every card, which turns a proof row into a menu
- Centred everything

---

## `02-card-anatomy.png` — label, rule, content, tag

**Take:**
- The card anatomy: heading, hairline rule, content, then a pill tag carrying real data. Adapted here so the price pill becomes a service count
- The thin rule as the divider inside a card rather than a border around it

**Do not take:**
- The brown and gold palette
- The duplicated label above and below the image
- Lorem ipsum density — real service names are longer and need more room than the reference allows

---

## `03-structure-and-hairlines.png` — the structural spine

The most useful of the three, and the source of the page's underlying structure.

**Take:**
- Section headings in a sticky left column with content running beside them, instead of centred above it
- Hairline rules separating list rows, no boxes
- A monochrome logo row between two hairlines
- A large rounded portrait with a circular badge overlapping its corner
- Restraint: near-monochrome, generous whitespace, borders only where they carry information

**Do not take:**
- The circled icon on every list row. That pattern implies a sequence, and Prospect's deadline list is not one — the date carries the information instead
- The carousel arrows; the service categories are a complete set, not a browse
- The grey-on-grey text; this project's grey is body copy only
